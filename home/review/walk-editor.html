    Drag a piece to move it; its corners to scale it; its edges to crop it (⌥ on a corner crops two edges); the knob above it to turn it (⇧ snaps to 15°).

    ⌥-drag a piece to slide its image under its crop.

    Locked pieces (the map) select only from the list. ←↑→↓ nudge (⇧ by 10), [ ] reorder, Esc deselects.
